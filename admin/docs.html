Reviewing posts

The Require approval for new posts switch at the top of the Posts page controls how new posts are handled:

  • Off (recommended at launch): new posts go live in the feed straight away. Moderate them afterwards from Posts → Approved or when they're reported.
  • On: every new post starts as Pending and stays hidden until approved. Open Posts → Pending to see the queue, oldest first.

Whichever mode you use:

  • Approve publishes a pending post at the top of the feed and starts its 30-day timer.
  • Reject asks for a reason, which is emailed to the author, and removes the post from the feed if it was already live.
  • Tick several posts to approve, reject, feature or delete them in one go.
Check the media

Click a post title to preview every photo and the video before approving.

Featuring posts

Click the star to pin a post in the Featured section above the chronological feed. Featuring a pending post approves it automatically. Click the star again to unfeature. Keep it to one or two posts at a time.

Deleting posts

The delete action permanently removes the post, all original photos and videos, and every generated thumbnail from storage. It cannot be undone, so you'll always be asked to confirm.

Handling reports

Members can report any live post. Reports for the same post are grouped, so the Reports column shows how many people flagged it.

  • Dismiss when the post doesn't break the rules. It stays live.
  • Remove post when it does. The post and media are deleted and the author is notified.
  • Reporter identities are visible to admins only, never to the post author.

30-day auto cleanup

Posts are deleted automatically 30 days after they go live by the Laravel Scheduler. The dashboard shows when each job last ran. On Hostinger, a single cron job runs the scheduler every minute:

* * * * * cd /home/USER/domains/feedeeforums.com/laravel && php artisan schedule:run >> /dev/null 2>&1

If the dashboard shows a job that hasn't run recently, check the cron entry in hPanel → Advanced → Cron Jobs. You can also use Run now on the dashboard to trigger cleanup manually.

Good moderation

  • Aim to clear the pending queue at least twice a day.
  • Always choose an accurate rejection reason so authors can fix and resubmit.
  • When unsure, leave a post pending and ask another admin.
  • Never share reporter details or private member information.
